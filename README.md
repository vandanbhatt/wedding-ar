# Vandan & Riddhi – 3D Wedding AR Experience 💍✨

A luxury 2.5D holographic Augmented Reality wedding experience designed for **Vandan & Riddhi's Save-the-Date Card** (29.12.2027).

Guests can tap their phone against the physical NFC PVC card or scan the back-of-card QR code to experience Vandan and Riddhi popping out in full 3D right from the printed floral arch—directly in mobile browser WebAR (no app download required).

## 💎 Photographic Identity & Quality
- **100% Original Faces Preserved:** Vandan and Riddhi's faces, smiles, skin tones, hair details, outfits, and traditional jewelry have been preserved bit-for-bit from the original photograph (`JAI_3712.JPG`).
- **No AI Facial Generation:** No synthetic redraws, AI faces, or beauty retouching.
- **Neural Alpha Extraction:** Clean background separation leaving original RGB pixels completely unaltered.

## 📦 Repository Structure
- `index.html`: Interactive 3D WebAR viewer supporting WebXR (Android Chrome) and Quick Look (iOS Safari).
- `output/vandan_riddhi_card_3D_AR.glb`: Ultralight **596 KB** card-matched 3D model with calibrated depth.
- `output/vandan_riddhi_3D_AR.glb`: Deluxe **8.2 MB** 2.5D garden photo model with champagne-gold frame and backing.
- `output/vandan_riddhi_card_back_with_qr.png`: Print-ready card back with high-resolution QR code pointing to this site.
- `output/vandan_riddhi_cutout.png`: Full-resolution isolated couple PNG ($3712 \times 5568$).
- `output/vandan_riddhi_card_3D_AR.blend`: Native Blender 5.3 scene file for the card pop-out.
- `output/vandan_riddhi_3D_AR.blend`: Native Blender 5.3 scene file for the deluxe photo frame.
